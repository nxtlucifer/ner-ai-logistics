The one rule that shapes every layer below

Roads are treated as changeably passable, not as fixed lines on a map. A corridor that was open this morning may be closed by a landslide this afternoon and open again tomorrow. Every layer of this stack exists to carry that fact accurately: the database stores road condition as dated evidence rather than as a property, the engines refuse a corridor rather than scoring it low, and the clients display a missing input as missing rather than as a safe one.

1What the platform does

The operating problem in the North East, and the six capabilities that answer it

Freight into and across the eight North Eastern states passes through a 22 km chokepoint at Siliguri, over single corridors whose next alternative is often more than 100 km away, through a monsoon that closes highways from May to September, and across cellular dead zones where a truck is simply unreachable for hours. A fleet manager's best instrument today is a phone call, and fuel is budgeted by a rule of thumb that models neither gradient nor payload.

The six capabilities

Accessibility intelligence
Weather, landslide exposure and closure evidence are combined per corridor and re-evaluated live, so the question is never "what is the shortest road" but "which of these roads is usable right now, and on what evidence".
Three-corridor explainable comparison
Primary, fuel-saving and backup corridors compared side by side on distance, ETA, litres, CO2 and reason-coded risk — with the deltas stated in points, minutes and kilometres rather than as a percentage of an ordinal score.
Physics-based fuel and emissions
Litres and CO2 derived from payload mass, cumulative climb metres and speed through a CMEM-form model with published coefficients — not from distance multiplied by a fleet average.
Fleet Sentinel escalation
A truck that has not moved beyond a PostGIS radius for a set interval and whose driver has not answered produces a populated SOS briefing to the manager automatically. The escalation is a rule, so it fires whether or not anyone was watching.
Offline-first driver client
Route geometry, turn maneuvers, roadside POIs and the emergency guide are cached before the dead zone; GPS fixes queue locally and replay idempotently on (trip_id, device_fix_id) when signal returns.
Vernacular in-cab assistance
Reason codes — not sentences — cross the wire, so Assamese, Bengali, Hindi and English guidance is produced by lookup rather than translation, and works with no model and no network.

Four design commitments that a judge can check in the code

CommitmentWhat it means in the implementation
Silence is not evidence No amount of elapsed time may move a road segment toward VERIFIED_OPEN. A repair claim is a distinct state (REPAIR_REPORTED) from a confirmation. Enforced in domain/road_memory.py.
A closed road is refused, not penalised route_eligibility runs before ranking and returns REJECTED. A penalty can be out-voted by other components; a refusal cannot.
UNKNOWN ≠ SAFE, STALE ≠ LIVE A factor with no usable reading is reported as NOT_AVAILABLE with a reason code. Stale observations are counted and named, never scored. A provider outage can never become risk = 0.
AI is never in the safety path Every engine is a deterministic weighted rule with published constants. A test asserts that no confidence, probability or model_version field ever reaches the wire. The language model only rewords facts the rules already produced.
Why deterministic rules are the engineering answer here, not a compromise

After a real incident, "the truck had not moved more than 50 m in 60 minutes and the driver did not answer in 30" is a defensible sentence in an inquiry. "The model scored 0.83" is not. Every constant in this system is published, versioned and arguable in review — and the append-only evidence log the platform produces is precisely the dataset that any future statistical model would have to beat.

2System architecture

Two purpose-built clients, one API, one data platform, one intelligence plane

The manager works at a desk with several screens and a keyboard. The driver works one-handed in a cab, often at night, frequently with no signal. The architectural rule that makes this testable

app/domain/ imports nothing

app/domain/ contains no import of SQLAlchemy, httpx or FastAPI. Every engine is a pure function over dataclasses. That is why risk, fuel, eligibility and reroute logic can be exercised exhaustively without a database or a network, why the constants can be argued with in review, and why the same logic is guaranteed identical whether it was reached from the manager console, the Android client or a direct API call.

Why clients never touch the database directly

Capacity rules, document enforcement and the safety path are only unbypassable if there is exactly one way to write. No client holds a database credential; Row Level Security is enabled on every table; and operations that must be atomic across several writes are server-side RPCs rather than a sequence of client calls that a dropped connection can leave half-applied.

3How one trip flows, end to end

Eight stages, from the manager's plan to proof of delivery — with the real call at each step

This is the spine of the product. Everything in sections 4 to 9 exists to serve one of these eight stages. The scenario: a 14-tonne consignment from Guwahati to Shillong during monsoon.

1
PLAN
Shipment, cargo and stops created atomically with capacity validation
2
PREDICT
Corridors generated; weather, landslide and climb evidence gathered per candidate
3
EXPLAIN
Three corridors compared with reason codes, litres and named gaps
4
AUTHORIZE
Reviewer token issued and consumed; the switch is one transaction
5
NAVIGATE
Geometry and maneuvers bound to a route revision, cached for the dead zone
6
TRACK
GPS ingested idempotently; freshness classified LIVE / STALE / NO_CONTACT
7
ADAPT
Conditions re-assessed; NO_ACTION, ALERT_ONLY or PROPOSE — never silent
8
DELIVER
Stops completed, proof captured, trip closed, audit sealed

Stage by stage, with the actual call

1
ManagerTrips page
Enters pickup, destination and cargo. POST /api/trips/plan → Supabase RPC plan_trip creates shipment, cargo items, trip and stops in one transaction, validating that total cargo mass does not exceed the assigned truck's rated capacity. Over-capacity is refused here, before dispatch — not discovered at the weighbridge.
2
Platformrouting + evidence
OSRM returns three candidate corridors with geometry, duration and turn maneuvers. For each, Open-Meteo is sampled at points along the polyline (not just at the endpoints), SRTM supplies cumulative climb metres, and landslide exposure is looked up. Each reading carries a freshness stamp. Stored as trip_routes rows with geography(LineString,4326) geometry.
3
Platformdomain engines
route_eligibility runs first — a confirmed closure returns REJECTED and the corridor leaves the comparison entirely. route_risk scores the survivors; fuel_model computes litres and CO2; route_recommendation applies an evidence-parity gate and refuses to compare two corridors that were scored from different available inputs. GET /api/trips/{id}/routes/recommendation
4
ManagerRoute review
Sees three corridors with distance, ETA, litres, CO2, a risk band, the reason codes behind it, and — explicitly — any factor that could not be obtained. Selecting a corridor that requires review consumes a single-use 30-minute token bound to a digest of the exact evidence shown. POST /api/trips/{id}/routes/{rid}/select → RPC select_route: demote, promote and re-point the trip in one transaction.
5
DriverAndroid client
Accepts the dispatched trip, verifies the truck (photo, registration, odometer, fuel level, damage notes), then downloads the navigation and offline packages — GET /api/driver/me/trip/navigation and .../offline-package — carrying geometry, maneuvers, stops, POIs and the emergency guide. Both are bound to a route revision, so a reroute landing mid-request can never draw a new line with old turns.
6
Driver → Platformtelemetry
POST /api/driver/me/location streams GPS fixes, batched and de-duplicated on device, idempotent on (trip_id, device_fix_id) so a replayed offline queue cannot double-count. telemetry_policy classifies each truck LIVE / STALE / NO_CONTACT by fix age; route_progress computes progress along the selected corridor.
7
Fleet Sentinelsafety core
A PostGIS ST_DWithin check establishes that the vehicle has not left a radius for the configured interval. Stationary and unanswered escalates to a populated SOS briefing — last known position, cargo, driver contact, elapsed silence — delivered to the manager by rule rather than by anyone happening to look.
8
Platform → Managerreroute
GET /api/trips/{id}/reroute returns exactly one of NO_ACTION, ALERT_ONLY or PROPOSE. ALERT_ONLY exists because most of the North East is a single corridor: "this road has deteriorated and there is nothing better" is real, actionable information. A proposal requires human acceptance — POST .../reroute/accept — so the driver's map never changes underneath them.
9
Driverdelivery
Arrive and complete each stop, then complete the trip. Every transition is checked against trip_state's legal-transition table, written to the append-only trip_events log with an optional location, and mirrored into audit_logs with actor, before/after JSON, reason and IP.
What makes this flow survive the North East specifically

Three properties, none of them cosmetic. Atomicity — plan, dispatch and route switch are single transactions, so a dropped connection cannot leave the manager looking at corridor B while the driver is authoritatively on corridor A. Idempotency — a replayed GPS queue produces the same state as a live stream. Revision binding — geometry and maneuvers are stamped with the route revision they belong to, and a client discards a package whose route no longer matches.

The release gate — the control that actually guarantees a field-usable APK

EXPO_PUBLIC_API_BASE_URL means "the laptop on the LAN". The build gate refuses to build when it is set, so a laptop-dependent APK cannot ship. The hosted intelligence origin has its own variable, EXPO_PUBLIC_INTELLIGENCE_BASE_URL, held to a stricter rule: a private network address, a plain-http origin or a loopback host is rejected at build time. The rule lives once, in releaseConfig.mjs, shared by the gate and the runtime — because a rule restated in two places is a rule that drifts.

Why the route switch is a database function, not three client writes

Selecting a corridor means three changes: demote the previously selected route, promote the new one, and re-point trips.selected_route_id. Performed as independent writes from a browser, each can fail or be lost separately — producing zero selected routes, two selected routes, or a trip whose pointer disagrees with its own route table. On a platform whose entire subject is weak connectivity, a half-applied route change is the one outcome that must be impossible. select_route makes it one transaction, with the currently-selected route as the concurrency token so two managers acting at once cannot both succeed.

8Layer 5 — Accessibility intelligence

Ten deterministic engines with published constants — the core of the submission
The rule this whole layer is built around

Every engine below is a deterministic weighted rule with published constants. Each carries a version name that says so, and a test asserts that no confidence, probability or model-version field ever reaches the wire. The language model is a separate thing entirely: it rewords facts the engines have already produced, and it has no write path to any decision.

The engines

EngineKindMethod and published constants
route_riskWeighted rule Rain ≤ 45 pts · wind ≤ 25 · duration ≤ 20 · distance ≤ 10, plus landslide points. Bands: LOW < 30 ≤ MODERATE < 60 ≤ HIGH. Thresholds: rain moderate 2.5 mm, heavy 7.5 mm; gust notable 40 km/h, severe 60 km/h. Stale observations are counted and reported, never scored.
route_eligibilityRefusal gate Runs before ranking. A confirmed closure is REJECTED, not penalised — a penalty can be out-voted by other components, a refusal cannot. Unknown landslide evidence forces REQUIRES_REVIEW.

(CMEM / SAE J1321)		 F = (B + K_payload·M + K_speed·max(0, v−50))·D + K_grade·max(0, ΔH) with B = 0.22 L/km, K_payload = 0.0075 L/(t·km), K_grade = 0.00018 L/m, K_speed = 0.002. CO2 at 2.68 kg/L.

state machine		 Five states: VERIFIED_OPEN · REPORTED_INCIDENT · CLOSED · REPAIR_REPORTED · AWAITING_VERIFICATION. Silence is not evidence — no elapsed time may move a road toward VERIFIED_OPEN. Evidence carries a source; FLEET_TRAVERSAL is the strongest.
monsoon_riskWeighted rule Recurrence · rainfall · season · last observed road status. Passability is a separate three-valued field, not a score, so a closed segment cannot narrowly lose a comparison.

Evidence integrity — how the risk response is shaped

A RouteRisk never returns a bare number. It carries inputs (each factor marked AVAILABLE or NOT_AVAILABLE), unavailable, reason_codes, observations_used and observations_stale alongside score — and the manager UI renders the gap next to the score it is missing from. The reason-code catalogue carries the exact semantic the problem statement requires:

Reason code — WEATHER_UNAVAILABLE

"Hazard data for this route is unavailable. This is not a clear route — it is an unchecked one."

When no plane is reachable, candidates return score: null, band: 'UNASSESSED', eligibility: 'NOT_ASSESSED'. Null rather than zero is the entire point: zero is a legitimate assessed value meaning "we looked and found nothing wrong". Null means nobody looked. The two must never render identically.

Reason codes, not sentences — how vernacular support actually works

The API sends HEAVY_RAIN_ON_CORRIDOR, not "heavy rain on the corridor". The client resolves it through i18n/reasonCodes.json. That single decision is what makes Assamese, Bengali, Hindi and English in-cab guidance a lookup rather than a translation call — so it is instant, identical across clients, reviewable by a human translator, and works with no model and no network.

The provider contract, in one line

A provider failure is displayed as a provider failure. It is never converted into a number. UNKNOWN ≠ SAFE and STALE ≠ LIVE are enforced in the domain layer, not left to the UI to remember.

Hazard and closure evidence

Landslide hazard, flood and damage reporting, and road closure bulletins enter the platform through road_memory — the five-state evidence machine — rather than through a score. Each item of evidence carries its source, its date and its state, and the strongest source is a FLEET_TRAVERSAL: one of our own trucks having driven the segment.

SourceContributes
Geological Survey of India · bhukosh.gsi.gov.inLandslide inventory and hazard exposure per zone

Our own research finding, and the design it produced

No Indian source publishes road reopening. GSI forecasts hazard, ASDMA reports damage, IMD reports rain — but reopening is decided by PWD, NHAI or BRO and communicated ad hoc. That single finding is why our own fleet traversals are treated as the strongest evidence a segment is open, and why the road-evidence log is append-only and per segment: over a season of operation, the platform accumulates the passability record for the region that no source currently holds.

Roles are read from the database, never from the token

Why this matters more than it sounds

A federated identity provider says authenticated for everyone. deps.get_current_user therefore reads the real role from the users table on every request. The consequence is operational, not theoretical: a demotion or a deactivation takes effect immediately, rather than whenever the user's token happens to expire. A revoked reviewer cannot authorise a corridor with a token issued three minutes ago.

Structural guards — misconfiguration fails loudly rather than quietly

GuardBehaviour
Secret hygieneThe backend refuses to start with the placeholder SECRET_KEY when APP_ENV is not development. VITE_* and EXPO_PUBLIC_* are inlined into client bundles and are therefore treated as public — no secret may use those prefixes.
Provider misconfigurationDATABASE_PROVIDER=supabase with a localhost URL is rejected at startup rather than silently serving a different database

Why each significant choice was made

ChoiceReasoning
Two apps, one backendThe manager works at a desk with several screens; the driver works one-handed in a cab with poor signal, sometimes at night. One responsive web app would compromise both.
Clients never touch the databaseCapacity rules, document enforcement and the safety path are only unbypassable if there is exactly one way to write.
PostgreSQL + PostGISCorridor geometry, breadcrumb tracks and ST_DWithin stationary detection are first-class database operations, not application loops.
ap-south-1Managed Postgres with PostGIS, edge functions and RLS, hosted in India — which matters for a government-sponsored logistics dataset.
psycopg 3, one driverServes both the async application engine and Alembic's sync engine from a single connection URL, so there is no second driver to keep in sync.
Python 3.11, not 3.14The analytics-phase dependencies do not yet publish 3.14 wheels. pyproject.toml pins >=3.11,<3.13 deliberately.
Start via run.pyOn Windows the asyncio selector policy must be set before uvicorn creates its event loop, or every database call fails with a psycopg InterfaceError.
MapLibre + OpenStreetMapLicence-free, self-hostable, and no per-map-load billing that would make a fleet console expensive to leave open all day.
Expo / React NativeOne Android build from one TypeScript codebase, and react-native-web makes the same screens testable in CI without a device farm.
Stateless intelligence serviceIt holds no state of its own, so it can be scaled, moved or restarted freely — and every other workflow keeps working regardless.
Deterministic enginesPublished constants can be argued with in review and defended in an inquiry. Every route decision carries the evidence it was made from — which is also exactly the dataset any future statistical model would have to beat.
No language model in the safety pathAfter a real incident, "the truck had not moved more than 50 m in 60 minutes and the driver did not answer in 30" is defensible. "The model scored 0.83" is not.

Scaling characteristics

Cost scales with trucks
Not with kilometres of road. All data sources are free and licence-clear; managed Postgres plus a container backend is the whole bill. No GPU, no on-prem server, no vehicle retrofit.
Geography is configuration
Only the hazard feeds are regional. The same schema, the same engines and the same clients serve Himalayan, Western Ghat and island logistics unchanged.
Horizontally scalable
The API is stateless and the intelligence service holds no state, so both scale by replica count. PostGIS indexes the geometry; corridor and stationary queries stay in the database.
